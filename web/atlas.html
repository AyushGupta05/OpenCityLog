Methodology · v0.4

A city change atlas, grounded in evidence — not forecasts.

OpenCityLog indexes observed urban changes from primary sources — planning records, official open data, Translink and DfI public project pages, and OpenStreetMap mapped visibility. Each record is timestamped and traceable to a primary source.

What counts as a "change"

Physical interventions (new stations, openings, demolitions), official decisions and route changes, and observed mapped-visibility records from OSM. Decision records are not treated as proof of later built outcomes unless the source documents that outcome.

Confidence ratings

Documented — at least one primary public source. Inferred — OSM mapped-visibility metadata (visibility on the map, not the same as construction date). Filter on the left panel to scope.

What we explicitly do not do

We do not forecast. We do not claim causation. Co-occurrence in space and time is descriptive only. OSM edit dates are mapped-visibility dates, not change-effective dates.

Evidence exports

Filtered CSV, GeoJSON, and selected-record briefs include dates, confidence, source rows, licences, caveats, and correction-path metadata.

Governance

OpenCityLog is an open-source urban changelog. Source data is licensed under permissive open licences (CC-BY 4.0, OGL, ODbL) where possible; contested changes are dual-flagged.
