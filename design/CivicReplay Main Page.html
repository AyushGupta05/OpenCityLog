Audience framing

Different stakeholders, one shared evidence surface.

The current app already supports a strong analyst workflow. The homepage now explains who benefits from that workflow and why.

Who this helps

A clearer story for planners, researchers, journalists, and public-interest teams.

  • Planners can compare districts without collapsing evidence into a black-box score.
  • Journalists can trace public claims back to dated, exportable source briefs.
  • Researchers can scan patterns over time, then drill into confidence and limitations.
  • Communities can inspect what changed in their area using visible provenance rather than opaque dashboards.
Visual direction

Warm civic-tech instead of cold dashboard blue.

This concept keeps the repo’s trust and map DNA, but introduces more grounded neutrals, moss green, rust, and parchment tones so the site feels more human and place-aware.
