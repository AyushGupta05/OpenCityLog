# Round119 Belfast Public Facilities More

Scope: official or near-official Belfast public/institutional architecture and city-change milestones from 2008-01-01 through 2026-05-19, focused on council/community facilities, education, health, transport buildings, culture, and heritage restoration. This scratch pass writes only under `tmp/subagents/round119_belfast_public_facilities_more`.

## Method

- Checked the curated manual architecture milestone corpus and prior tmp/subagent outputs for duplicate titles, source URLs, source record IDs, and facility names.
- Prioritized official sources: Translink and the Department of Education.
- Kept source claims narrow: observed facility/service/building milestones only, with no causal or outcome claims.
- Geometry is approximate because the official pages used here do not publish GIS geometry. Station points were manually geocoded and cross-checked against local OSM-derived transport stop points; Colaiste Feirste uses the existing curated Beechmount/Sportlann point already in the corpus.

## Limitations

- The Translink anniversary page is retrospective. It is suitable for documented station milestones, but not a substitute for original project completion files.
- The Great Victoria Street source documents closure and passenger arrangements, not demolition, disposal, or future redevelopment.
- The Department of Education Colaiste Feirste page is a construction-start/progress source. It should not be treated as completion or opening evidence.
- No candidate in this scratch artifact claims prediction, causality, usage outcomes, or service quality outcomes.
