## Caveats

HED listing/de-listing dates are stronger than HARNI narrative dates. HARNI condition/risk notes are external visual inspection/status evidence and should be displayed as caveated observations, not as full condition surveys or proof of restoration outcome unless the page explicitly says restoration was complete.

Coordinates are approximate review points. HED records include Irish Grid references, but the WGS84 points in this scratch file should be rechecked with a reproducible CRS transform before ingestion.
