The applicant proposes: Variation of conditions 14 ( Permitted Timeline) and 15 (Phasing) of planning
permission Ref: 2373/APP/2005/2815 dated 12/12/2006 (Extraction of sand and gravel;backfilling

notice and section 106 agreement.
