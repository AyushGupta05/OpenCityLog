I refer to your letter of 25 October 2006, consulting the Mayor of London on the above planning 
application.  On 21 December 2006, the Mayor considered a report on this proposal, reference 
PDU/1559/01.  A copy of the report is attached, in full. 
Having considered the report, the Mayor considered that residential led mixed use development is 
appropriate for this location, however, a considerable amount of further work is required before a 
decision can be made. 
  
The attached report identifies the key strategic issues that require further detailed work.  This 
covers the principle of the proposed density, transport matters, urban design, energy, affordable 
housing and mix and the provision of play space and equipment for the under five age group. 
 
If Tower Hamlets Council decides in due course that it is minded to approve the application, it 
should allow the Mayor fourteen days to decide whether or not to direct the Council to refuse 
planning permission (under article 4(1)(b)(i) of the Town & Country Planning (Mayor of London) 
Order 2000).  You should therefore send me a copy of any officer’s report on this case to your 
planning committee (or its equivalent), together with a statement of the permission your authority 
proposes to grant and of any conditions the authority proposes to impose, and a copy of any 
representations made in respect of the application (article 4(1)(a) of the Order).
