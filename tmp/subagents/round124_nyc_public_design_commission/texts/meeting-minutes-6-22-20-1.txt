CERTIFICATE  27532 
 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the construction of a 
café addition, storage building, and adjacent site work, McCarren Park House, McCarren Park, 
between Lorimer Street, Bedford Avenue, and Driggs Avenue, Brooklyn, submitted by the 
Department of Parks & Recreation, represented by exhibits 580-CO, CP & CQ of record in this 
matter, hereby gives to the same unanimous preliminary approval with the understanding that 
the team will conduct paint removal tests to determine the color of the original brick façade 
and submit the results of the findings along with alternative paint color options at final review. 
The Commission urges the team to consider colors for the café addition, window mullions, and 
storage building that complement the historic brick instead of matching it. The Commission 
also encourages the Department of Parks & Recreation to replace the 1930s era roof with one 
that more closely matches the original roof design in a future project. 
 
Preliminary approval is conditioned upon commencement of work before June 22, 2022 and 
submission of this project for final review and approval.

CERTIFICATE  27533 
 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the reconstruction of 
a portion of Kelly Park Playground, East 14th Street and Avenue S, Brooklyn, submitted by the 
Department of Parks & Recreation, represented by exhibits 2346-P, Q & R of record in this 
matter, hereby gives to the same unanimous preliminary approval with the understanding that 
(1) the existing bear sculpture will be relocated to the 2-to-5-year-old play area and (2) the 
fence diagram will accurately illustrate the openings at the connections from the play 
equipment to the central raised platform in the 2-to-5 and 5-to-12 year old play area.   
 
Preliminary approval is conditioned upon commencement of work before June 22, 2022 and 
submission of this project for final review and approval.
