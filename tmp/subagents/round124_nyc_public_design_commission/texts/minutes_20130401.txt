CERTIFICATE 24696 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered a proposal for the installation of 
Waterfront Nature Walk by George Trakas, Phases II & Ill, Newtown Creek Water Pollution 
Control Plant, 329 Greenpoint Avenue, Brooklyn, submitted by the Department of Cultural 
Affairs' Percent for Art Program, the Department of Design and Construction and the 
Department of Environmental Protection, represented by exhibits 2421-DO, DP, DQ, DR & DS 
of record in this matter, hereby gives to the same unanimous conceptual approval with the 
understanding that (1) the design of the drinking fountain in the center of the shelter will be 
submitted; (2) all of the signage, including for the tree fossils and waterways, will also be 
provided; (3) the Phase I planting that was eaten by wildlife will be replaced with the 
recommended species as part of the current project; and (4) the Department of Environmental 
Protection's Bureau of Wastewater Treatment will contract with a qualified vendor to maintain 
all of Waterfront Nature Walk in perpetuity. 
Conceptual approval is conditioned upon submission of this project for preliminary review and 
approval before April 1, 2015. 
A true copy of resolution adopted by 
the Design Commission at its meeting 
on April 1, 2013.
