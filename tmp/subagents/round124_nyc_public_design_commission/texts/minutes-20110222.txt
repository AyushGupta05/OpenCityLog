RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the reconstruction of

RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the installation of an 
interpretive signage system, 34th Street Partnership, Tenth Avenue, 30th Street, Park Avenue 
and 36th Street, Manhattan, submitted by the Department of Transportation, represented by

the Commission will be incorporated into the first three signs; (3) the final layout, text and 
location for the remaining 18 signs will be submitted for committee review prior to developing 
final drawings; and (4) the final design will serve as a prototype for use throughout the City. The 
Commission also requests that the 34th Street Partnership survey the existing signage, and 
remove any that is superfluous. 
Preliminary approval is conditioned upon submission of this project for final review and 
approval before February 22, 2013.
