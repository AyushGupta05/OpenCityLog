CERTIFICATE  28870 
 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the reconstruction of 
Canarsie Branch Library, 1580 Rockaway Parkway, Brooklyn, submitted by the Brooklyn Public 
Library, represented by exhibits 7411-E, F, G, H & I of record in this matter, hereby gives to the 
same unanimous preliminary approval with the understanding that the team will provide an 
interim submission before returning for final review that includes (1) confirmation that Brooklyn 
Public Library has coordinated the artwork with the Department of Cultural Affairs’ Percent for 
Art team, in accordance with the Percent for Art program requirements; (2) physical samples of 
the façade and screening materials; (3) a comprehensive review of the proposed black locust 
wood pavers and permeable joint filler, including (a) examples of their use within a busy, urban 
public realm with major climate concerns and (b) alternative paving materials, in case sufficient 
evidence of their use in a similar setting does not exist; and (4) studies of the entry walkway, 
including (a) use of a material that increases friction and slip resistance, (b) a single, even slope 
along the full length of the ramp, and (c) the addition of a handrail to further improve access 
and comfort for people of all abilities. 
 
 
Preliminary approval is conditioned upon commencement of work before June 18, 2026 and 
submission of this project for final review and approval. 
 
 
 
 
A true copy of resolution adopted by 
the Design Commission at its meeting  
on June 18, 2024.
