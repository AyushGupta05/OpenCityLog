CERTIFICATE  26127 
 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the rehabilitation of 
La Marqueta and La Placita, Park Avenue between East 114th Street and East 116th Street, 
Manhattan, submitted by the Economic Development Corporation, represented by exhibits 
1925-AT, AY, AV & AW of record in this matter, hereby gives to the same unanimous 
preliminary approval with the understanding that, before returning for final review, the team will 
restudy the color palette and submit a revised proposal that (1) includes the Benjamin Moore 
color codes that most closely resemble the existing viaduct color; (2) replaces ‘Old Straw Hat’ 
yellow with a gray similar to Benjamin Moore 2130-40 ‘Black Pepper Gray,’ HC-145 ‘Van 
Courtland Blue’ or the precedent image on Slide 22 of the presentation; (3) replaces the metal 
panel with a brighter orange hue such as Benjamin Moore 2012-10 ‘Tawny Day Lily’ or 2012-
20 ‘Flame;’ (4) eliminates the painted Hartford Green reveal; and (5) paints the roof fascia either 
gray to match the garage door frames or bright orange tone to match the metal panel. 
Approval is also given with the understanding that a signage proposal will be submitted in Fall 
2017.  
 
Preliminary approval is conditioned upon submission of this project for final review and 
approval before April 24, 2019.
