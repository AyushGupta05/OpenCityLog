CERTIFICATE 23941 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the 
reconstruction of the park perimeter, including the relocation of nine monuments, as part 
of the master plan, Battery Park, Battery Place, State Street, Whitehall Street, and the 
Hudson River, Manhattan, submitted by the Department of Parks & Recreation, 
represented by exhibits 1371-JP, JQ, JR & JS of record in this matter, hereby gives to 
the same unanimous preliminary approval with the understanding that (1) individual 
conservation and relocation proposals will be submitted for each of the nine monuments 
and include (a) thorough current condition reports, (b) detailed methods and materials 
for all proposed procedures, and (c) rationalizations for any replications; and (2) once 
the Battery Conservancy coordinates with the Battery Park City Authority and the Coast 
Guard, it will submit relocation proposals for the two outstanding monuments, Giovanni 
da Verrazzano (1909) by Ettore Ximenes and the Coast Guard Memorial (1947) by 
Norman M. Thomas. 
Preliminary approval is conditioned upon submission of this project for final review and 
approval before July 19, 2012. 
A true copy of resolution adopted by 
the Design Commission at its meeting 
on July 19, 2010.
