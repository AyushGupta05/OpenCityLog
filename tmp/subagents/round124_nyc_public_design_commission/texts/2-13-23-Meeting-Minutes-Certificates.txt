CERTIFICATE  28374 
 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the installation of a 
marker (First Portable Cell Phone Call), Sixth Avenue between West 53rd Street and West 54th 
Street, Manhattan, submitted by the Department of Transportation, represented by exhibits 
7609-A, B & C of record in this matter, hereby tables the same without prejudice to a 
resubmission with the understanding that (1) the team provide context on existing markers, 
including how they are telling the story about New York City’s history, including technological 
innovations, and social and cultural events of New York City; (2) the message of the marker be 
refined to clarify what it is commemorating, including re-evaluating the inclusion of the creator, 
the team, and/or the company; (3) the graphic, typography, spacing, and how the date is 
incorporated in the text is re-studied; and (4) the location or narrative of the location of the 
plaque be re-studied to separate the marker from current technologies, which may not be 
related to the message of the marker. Commissioner Miranda, Commissioner Tisch, and 
Commissioner Van Bramer approved, not tabled.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
A true copy of resolution adopted by 
the Design Commission at its meeting  
on February 13, 2023.
