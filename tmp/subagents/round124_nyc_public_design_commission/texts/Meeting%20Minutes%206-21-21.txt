CERTIFICATE  27827 
 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the redesign of 
wayfinding and commemorative signage (Heritage Trails), Lower Manhattan, Manhattan, 
submitted by the Department of Transportation on behalf of the Downtown Alliance, 
represented by exhibits 5789-F, G, H, I, J, K, L, M, N & O of record in this matter, hereby 
rejects the same. The Commission expressed appreciation for the work undertaken by the 
Downtown Alliance and the stated goals of providing more inclusive signage, noting that the 
Heritage Trail commemorative markers are an opportunity for truth and reconciliation and a 
pivotal opportunity to take a wider lens and accurately tell the history of the area, including 
stories on the Native American and enslaved populations. The Commission recommended that 
(1) the Heritage Trail signage program be holistically restudied; (2) the text, images, and 
locations be developed by a team of historical experts with relevant and diverse backgrounds 
of study; (3) additional signs be considered to commemorate significant historical persons or 
events as close as possible to the related locations; and (4) the revised text, images, and 
locations be shared in full with the community board before any resubmission to the Public 
Design Commission.  
 
 
Preliminary approval is conditioned upon submission of this project for final review and 
approval before June 21, 2023.
