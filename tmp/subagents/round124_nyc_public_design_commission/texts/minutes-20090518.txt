reconstruction of a playground, including the construction of a comfort station, as part 
of the construction of the Coney Island Center, Asser Levy Park, Sea Breeze Avenue, 
West Fifth Street and Surf Avenue, Brooklyn, submitted by the Department of Design 
and Construction and the Department of Parks & Recreation, represented by exhibits 
1340-AO, AP, AQ & AR of record in this matter, hereby gives to the same unanimous 
preliminary approval with the understanding that the team will (1) minimize the distance 
and maximize the visibility between the tot lot and the play area for older children, to 
the extent possible; (2) consider connecting the climber and the spray feature for a 
more integrated play experience; (3) confirm that all paving, specifically the wood 
decking, is ADA-compliant; (4) modify the comfort station, utilizing glass block on the 
two end walls to provide light, and a butterfly roof to eliminate the awkward pitch and 
disguise the roof vent; (5) develop a "ribbon candy"-like perimeter fence more in 
keeping with the loops of the wicket; and (6) submit the memorial tree and plaque in 
recognition of the 4.5 million people brought to freedom by the Hebrew Immigrant Aid 
Society for formal review as a separate application since it was never submitted. 
Preliminary approval is conditioned upon submission of this project for final review and 
approval before May 18, 2011.
