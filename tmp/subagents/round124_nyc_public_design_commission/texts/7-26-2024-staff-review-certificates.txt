CERTIFICATE  28899 
 
RESOLVED That per the resolution of delegation, the executive director having considered 
designs for the minor modifications to the reconstruction of a salt shed and interim compost 
facility as a part of the Gowanus Canal combined sewer overflow (CSO) facility, 2nd Avenue 
and 6th Street, Brooklyn, submitted by the Department of Environmental Protection and the 
Department of Sanitation, represented by exhibits 7563-U & V of record in this matter, hereby 
gives to the same amended final approval on behalf of the Design Commission.   
 
Final approval is conditioned upon commencement of work before July 26, 2026, and 
submission of comprehensive photographic documentation of the completed project consisting 
of 8” x 10” archival-quality color photographs. 
 
 
 
 
A true copy of resolution adopted by 
the executive director on July 26, 2024. 
 
 
Sreoshy Banerjea 
Executive Director
