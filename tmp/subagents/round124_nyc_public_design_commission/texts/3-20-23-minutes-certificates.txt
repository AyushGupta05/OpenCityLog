CERTIFICATE  28406 
 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered a proposal for the conservation 
and short-term loan of Alamo (1967) by Tony Rosenthal, from Astor Place, Lafayette Street and 
East Eighth Street, Manhattan to the Artists Hall of Fame exhibit for one week, Hamptons Fine 
Art Fair, Southampton, New York, submitted by the Department of Transportation, represented 
by exhibits 3752-Q, R, S & T of record in this matter, hereby gives to the same unanimous 
preliminary approval with the understanding that the team will (1) study an alternative 
conservation solution where the structure is relieved internally, which will be submitted to the 
staff as an update with the preferred direction; (2) provide an update on a communications 
strategy and/or signage to explain the status of the artwork; and (3) per standard procedure, (a) 
provide a copy of the written agreement between the Department of Transportation and the 
Tony Rosenthal Estate and (b) a detailed maintenance plan, including a proposed schedule for 
the inspection and replacement of the Teflon and clarification on who is responsible for 
maintenance of this mechanism and its associated cost.  
 
 
 
 
A true copy of resolution adopted by 
the Design Commission at its meeting  
on March 20, 2023.
