CERTIFICATE  30255 
 
The Design Commission, having considered designs for the construction of a skybridge over 
33rd Street Plaza, connecting 1 Pennsylvania Plaza and 2 Pennsylvania Plaza, West 33rd 
Street and Seventh Avenue, Manhattan, submitted by the Department of Transportation, 
represented by exhibits 5852-CE, CF, CG, CH & CI of record in this matter, hereby conducted 
three public votes, during which a majority approval was not achieved. 
 
For the first round of voting, Commissioners Castilla, Maloney, Miranda, and Kiehl motioned and 
voted to table. Commissioner Armstead, Hawkinson, Heinzen, Marton, and Tisch voted not to 
table. The motion to table did not pass. 
 
For the second round of voting, Commissioners Heinzen, Maloney, Miranda, and Tisch 
motioned and voted to approve. Commissioners Armstead, Hawkinson, and Kiehl voted to 
reject. Commissioner Castilla voted to table. Chair Deborah Marton did not vote, as the final 
vote count for either option would not result in a majority.  
 
For the third round of voting, Commissioners Castilla, Heinzen, Maloney, Miranda, and Tisch 
motioned and voted to approve. Commissioners Armstead, Hawkinson, Kiehl, and Marton voted 
to reject. 
 
No majority was achieved with six affirmative votes, and accordingly the Commission failed to 
act on this submission at this meeting. However, the Commission’s time to act, consistent with 
section 855 of the New York City Charter has not yet elapsed, and thus this matter remains 
pending before the Commission.

on December 15, 2025.
