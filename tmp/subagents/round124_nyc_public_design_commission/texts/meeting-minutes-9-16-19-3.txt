RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the reconstruction of 
the coastline (East Side Coastal Resiliency) and East River Park, including the construction of 
three bridges, a comfort station, two recreation centers, and two gate houses, East River 
between Montgomery Street and East 25th Street, Manhattan, submitted by the Department of 
Design and Construction, the Department of Parks & Recreation, the Department of 
Environmental Protection, and the Department of Transportation, represented by exhibits 7368-
J, K, L, M, N & O of record in this matter, hereby gives to the same unanimous preliminary 
approval with the understanding that the team will (1) re-study the alignment of the pedestrian 
bridge arches, noting that the previous angle was a more successful design and an integrated 
lighting solution would be warranted for three new bridges; (2) clarify the shade strategy for East 
River Park, including the caliper and projected growth of new trees; (3) incorporate more 
nuance into the wall pattern, in particular at the pedestrian scale; (4) include solatubes to bring 
natural light into the park buildings; (5) provide technical details on the glass brick gate house 
designs; (6) develop the closure gate design as part of the graphic designer’s scope and from 
the public’s perspective; and (7) provide a detailed design for the East River Park amphitheater, 
ensuring that it is accessible and functional. The Commission encouraged the team to continue 
to study the possibility of phasing the construction and thoughtfully consider any interim design 
solutions. Commissioner Nielsen recused herself. 
 
Preliminary approval is conditioned upon submission of this project for final review and approval 
before September 16, 2021.
