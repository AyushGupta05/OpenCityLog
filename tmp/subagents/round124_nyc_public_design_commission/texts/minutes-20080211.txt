CERTIFICATE 23069 
RESOLVED That the Art Commission, having considered a proposal for the 
conservation of Truth and Research, two plaster reliefs (ca. 1900) attributed to Herbert 
Adams and Olin L. Warner, Erasmus Hall High School, 911 Flatbush Avenue, Brooklyn, 
submitted by the School Construction Authority, represented by exhibits 378-CK, CL & 
CM of record in this matter, hereby gives to the same unanimous preliminary approval 
with the understanding that, as the black paint may or may not be able to be removed 
without disturbing the original "bronze" finished surface, (1) tests, using various 
procedures and in various locations, be undertaken using cross sections to determine how 
much ofthe original frnish is below the black paint , if it is prudent to remove the black 
paint, and if so, what procedure(s) should be used to remove the paint; (2) once these 
tests are completed, and before any additional work is done, a site visit be organized for 
the Conservation Advisory Group to view the different tests and discuss the proposed 
procedures; (3) if it is determined that the black paint cannot be removed safely, a new 
restoration "bronze" surface will be applied to the black paint in a manner that rni1 1ics 
the original bronze surface; ( 4) if it is determined that the black paint can be removed 
safely, but the original surface has excessive loss, a barrier layer that protects and isolates 
what remains of the original surface from any added restoration surface will be applied to 
the original finish before an additional finish be added~ (5) if it is determined that the 
black paint can be removed safely and there is not excessive loss, the surface should be 
inpainted using a medium different from the original finish; and (6) the chronology 
should include more detailed information, such as exact dates for all of the past work and 
consultations, especially on the last page. 
Preliminary approval is conditioned upon the commencement of work before February 
I I, 2010.
