represented by exhibits 6685-L, M, N, O, P & Q of record in this matter, hereby gives to the 
same unanimous preliminary approval with the understanding that before returning for final, the 
team will (1) at the façade (a) further clarify where BIPV and porcelain panels are being located, 
suggesting that Department of Citywide Adminstrative Services (DCAS) and the solar consultant 
be further engaged to ensure that the panel locations are optimized for maximum energy 
generation, (b) explore light-colored alternatives for the porcelain panels, noting that a dark, low-
albedo tone is counterproductive to the team’s sustainability goals, and (c) clarify what material 
is being used at the location where the massing volume steps in; (2) further clarify (a) the 
window proportions, including fenestration details, (b) the function and detailing of the light wells 
and inset sills, (c) the detailing of the BIPV-integrated solar shade, and (d) how the 
prefabricated modular construction will impact and/or overlay with the fenestration locations; (3) 
provide an update on the forthcoming maintenance agreement with the DCAS, which should 
include a plan for panel maintenance or replacement that prevents an ad hoc appearance if 
BIPV panels lose functionality, noting that leaving defunct BIPV panels on the rainscreen is 
preferable to replacement with dark porcelain; and (4) continue to restudy the plant selections 
and adjacencies to address how (a) the sun-thriving understory species selected will survive 
under shaded trees, (b) the edible annual plants will be maintained, since they need to be 
replaced after they are harvested, and (c) the sedum will survive within planted areas with taller 
plants that require deeper soil depth, as these species do not typically mix well.  
 
Preliminary approval is conditioned upon submission of this project for final review and approval 
before July 17, 2025.
