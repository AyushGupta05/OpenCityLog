RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the construction of a 
greenway and daylighting of Tibbetts Brook, between West 230th Street and Hester and 
Piero’s Mill Pond, Van Cortlandt Park, Bronx, submitted by the Department of Environmental 
Protection and the Department of Parks & Recreation, represented by exhibits 1960-EB, EC, 
ED, EE, EF, EG & EH of record in this matter, hereby gives to the same unanimous preliminary 
approval with the understanding that prior to returning for final review, the team will (1) 
holistically refine the architectural elements along the greenway to make the space feel 
recognizable as a truly public, pedestrianized space, including by (a) reconsidering the 
suitability of the Davit pole for a pedestrian and cycling path, (b) considering the cohesion, 
detailing, and transparency of the railing design, decking, and gates across all different site 
widths and landscape types, particularly where the path is narrow and the railing could feel 
heavy, opaque, or constricting, and (c) thinking about the architectural identity and detailing 
along the length of the corridor and how it varies based on the width and context of the site; (2) 
update the presentation to provide (a) detailed cross sections showing the ways in which the 
structures, railings, lighting, furnishings, planting, and other elements relate to the varied site 
conditions along each segment of the project and (b) a phasing and construction sequencing 
diagram; (3) develop a signage strategy and graphic that pulls the design together across all of 
the landscape types and that (a) considers the experience of pedestrians accessing the 
greenway at different entry points and (b) relates to the NYC Parks graphic identity standards 
while also distinguishing itself as a unique greenway space; and (4) clarify the maintenance 
strategy further, including (a) whether the porous pavement will be regularly vacuumed, (b) 
whether a replanting budget can be reserved for the future, and (c) whether the design team 
can help NYC Parks and DEP to establish a maintenance guide for the site.   
 
Preliminary approval is conditioned upon submission of this project for final review and 
approval before August 14, 2025.
