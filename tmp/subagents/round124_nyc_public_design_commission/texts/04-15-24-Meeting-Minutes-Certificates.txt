CERTIFICATE  28802 
 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the reconstruction of 
the Brooklyn Detention Complex, 275 Atlantic Avenue, Brooklyn, submitted by the Department 
of Design and Construction and the Department of Correction, represented by exhibits 2692-CI, 
CJ, CK, CL, CM & CN of record in this matter, hereby gives to the same unanimous preliminary 
approval with the understanding that the team will, in an interim submission before returning for 
final review, (1) provide daylighting studies showing the quality of the natural light  and how it is 
impacted by the horizontal louvers at the recreation areas and housing units; (2) for the main 
entrance canopy, (a) reduce the width as much as possible, particularly at the corner condition, 
and simplify the channel detail and (b) explore alternative materials to faux wood that provide a 
sense of warmth at the soffit; (3) study the location of benches along State Street and remove 
the bench that is nested in the building niche; (4) for the site lighting, explore an integrated 
lighting design to reduce the number of different light fixtures; and (5) continue to refine and 
simplify the street tree palette based on the neighborhood context. 
 
Preliminary approval is conditioned upon submission of this project for final review and approval 
before April 15, 2026.

the Design Commission at its meeting  
on April 15, 2024.
