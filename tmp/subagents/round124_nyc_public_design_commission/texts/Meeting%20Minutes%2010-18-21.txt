CERTIFICATE  27899 
 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered a proposal for the long term loan 
of Thomas Jefferson (1833) by Pierre-Jean David (David D'Angers), City Council Chamber, City 
Hall to the New-York Historical Society, 170 Central Park West, Manhattan, submitted by the 
Department of Citywide Administrative Services, represented by exhibits 793-H & I of record in 
this matter, hereby gives to the same unanimous preliminary approval to the removal of the 
artwork from the City Council Chamber with the understanding that the location will be further 
studied and, before the end of 2021, an appropriate location will be determined where it will 
remain accessible to the public. 
 
  
The Commission notes the history of the artwork in City Hall, that it was gifted to the people of 
New York by one of the first Jewish officers in the U.S. military, Uriah Phillips Levy, in 
recognition of Thomas Jefferson’s commitment to religious freedom, and that Jefferson was the 
author of the Declaration of Independence, a founding document of the United States. The 
Commission also recognizes the layered narratives and meanings assigned to this artwork and 
takes seriously the implications of monumentalizing figures who, despite espousing the virtues 
of freedom and democracy, prospered from the labor of enslaved people and upheld racist 
beliefs to justify the institution of slavery. The Commission acknowledges the passionate call 
from members of the City Council to remove the artwork from their chambers and to install it in a 
place where it can be contextualized and serve as an educational tool. The Commission also 
thanks the New-York Historical Society for its ongoing commitment to providing opportunities for 
the public to learn about and discuss the history of New York City and the Country.  
 
 
A true copy of resolution adopted by

on October 18, 2021.
