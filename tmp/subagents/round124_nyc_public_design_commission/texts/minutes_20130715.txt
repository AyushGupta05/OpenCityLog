RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the reconstruction of 
the Riegelmann Boardwalk, Phase II, Brighton 15th Street to Coney Island Avenue, Brooklyn, 
submitted by the Department of Parks & Recreation, represented by exhibits 1170-IP, IQ, IR & 
IS of record in this matter, hereby gives to the same unanimous final approval subject to further 
study by the Parks Department of the concrete substructure in order to increase its porosity. 
The Commission also gives its approval with the understanding that the Parks Department will 
( 1) use high-friction coefficient recycled plastic lumber (RPL) with a 1 O' concrete carriage lane 
on Riegelmann Boardwalk moving forward, noting the importance of a consistent aesthetic 
treatment; and (2) if the slip resistance proves effective for non-vehicular uses, test the RPL for 
vehicular use with the goal of eliminating the concrete carriage lane altogether. 
The Commission also encourages the Parks Department to continue to work with RPL 
manufacturers to improve/test slip resistance as well as explore other sustainable materials for 
boardwalks, as they are developed. 
Final approval is conditioned upon commencement of work before July 15, 2015 and 
submission of comprehensive photographic documentation of the completed project consisting 
of 8" x 1 O" archival-quality color photographs.
