interactive touch screens in public payphone kiosks citywide, submitted by the Department of

aesthetic established by the City's Street Furniture Franchise; and (2) the prototype, once 
approved, should be installed throughout the City, including the outer boroughs, which are not 
as well represented in the pilot due to the locations of the existing double telephone kiosk, with 
the understanding that there are franchise limitations and other siting considerations. 
Final approval is conditioned upon commencement of work before November 15, 2012 and 
submission of a minimum of three (3) 8" x 1 0" color archival-quality photographs of the 
completed project.
