CERTIFICATE  28200 
 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered the installation of prototypical 
public communications structures (Link5G) in Residence Zoning and Historic Districts citywide, 
submitted by the Office of Technology and Innovation, represented by exhibits 6816-IY, IZ & 
JA of record in this matter, hereby gives unanimous preliminary and final approval. Approval is 
given with the understanding that the installations will be limited to a fixed number of pilot sites 
and that the team will (1) submit a detailed pilot proposal for approval, which will include a 
specified cap on the number of installations as well as location as soon as possible or on an 
ongoing basis when known, and siting guidelines of the poles; (2) (a) engage community 
leaders before installation to ensure that they are aware of the proposal, including the design of 
the poles and provisioning of service, and are given an opportunity to provide feedback, and 
(b) continue to work with the Commission to identify ways to gauge community acceptance of 
structures installed and activated as part of the pilot; (3) provide local and/or global examples 
of multi-tenant 5G pole structures sited on sidewalks in comparable dense urban 
neighborhoods with the goal of suggesting alternatives for Residence Zoning and Historic 
Districts; and (4) remove and replace the pilot installations in the event another design is 
selected for citywide installation in Residence Zoning and Historic Districts. The Commission 
recognizes the urgency and need for internet access in underserved areas.  
 
Final approval is conditioned upon submission of a proposal for a fixed number of sites 
before August 8, 2024.

on August 8, 2022.
