CERTIFICATE 23129 
RESOLVED That the Art Commission, having considered designs for the installation of 
a distinctive sidewalk, 10 West End A venue, Manhattan, submitted by the Department of 
Buildings, represented by exhibits 6903-A, B & C of record in this matter, hereby gives 
to the same unanimous preliminary and final approval with the understanding that the 
applicant will enlarge the tree pits to 15' x 5'. 
The Commission notes that it utilizes the same criteria to evaluate all distinctive sidewalk 
proposals, as described in its Submission Guidelines, and in this case, approves French 
Grey, rather than standard Department of Transportation grey, because (1) the scoring is 
standard 5' x 5'; (2) the large expanse of sidewalk, including the entire block from 59th 
Street to 60th Street, allows for some slight variation of the standard; and (3) the applicant 
will enlarge the tree pits to accommodate what the Commission would have required had 
this application been submitted as a distinctive sidewalk proposal prior to installation. 
Final approval is conditioned upon commencement of work before April 14, 2010 and 
submission of three (3) 8" x 10" color archival-quality photographs of the completed 
project.
