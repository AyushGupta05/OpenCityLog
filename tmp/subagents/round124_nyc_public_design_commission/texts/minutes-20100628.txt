CERTIFICATE 23935 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the installation 
of security measures, Williamsburg Bridge, Manhattan and Brooklyn, submitted by the 
Department of Transportation and the Department of Parks & Recreation, represented 
by exhibits 58-BU, BV, BW & BX of record in this matter, hereby gives to the same 
unanimous preliminary and final approval with the understanding that (1) the standard 
Parks Department tree protection notes will be incorporated into the contract drawings; 
and (2) Forestry will determine (a) whether the London Plane trees are damaged during 
construction and need to be replaced, (b) their replacement value, and (c) the correct 
species with which they should be replaced. The Commission reiterates its 
recommendation that the agencies specify that the London Plane trees should be 
replaced as their root systems will be severed during construction, making the trees 
unstable. 
Final approval is conditioned upon commencement of work before June 28, 2012 and 
submission of a minimum of three (3) 8" x 1 0" color archival-quality photographs of the 
completed project.
