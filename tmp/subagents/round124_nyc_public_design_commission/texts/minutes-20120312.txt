CERTIFICATE 24273 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the reconstruction of 
the Riegelmann Boardwalk, Phase II, Brighton Beach, Brighton 15th Street to Coney Island 
Avenue, Brooklyn, submitted by the Department of Parks & Recreation, represented by exhibits 
1170-GR, GS & GU of record in this matter, hereby gives to the same unanimous preliminary 
approval. Given the negative impact of deforestation on the environment, the Commission 
supports the use of sustainable materials, but recognizes that this is a transitional period. 
Consequently, approval is given with the understanding that the Parks Department will 
continue to (1) monitor the installation of the high friction coefficient recycled plastic lumber, 
and report back to the Commission on its findings; (2) continue to work with manufacturers to 
increase recycled plastic lumber's friction coefficient and develop alternative sustainable 
materials; (3) continue to pursue Black Locust, including exploring setting up its own Black 
Locust farm; (4) narrow the concrete lane to 1 0' (from 12'); (5) study moving the concrete lane 
inboard, and submit an alternative design(s) for committee review; and (6) test potential wood 
alternatives on this section of the boardwalk. 
Preliminary approval is conditioned upon submission of this project for final review and 
approval before March 12, 2014. 
A true copy of resolution adopted by 
the Design Commission at its meeting 
on March 12, 2012.
