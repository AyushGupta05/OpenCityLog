CERTIFICATE  27674 
 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered a proposal for the installation of 
an artwork by Kameelah Janan Rasheed, Willoughby Square Park, Willoughby Street, 
Abolitionist Place (Duffield Street) and Gold Street, Brooklyn, submitted by the Department of 
Cultural Affairs’ Percent for Art Program and the Economic Development Corporation, 
represented by exhibits 5587-JF, JG, JH & JI of record in this matter, hereby tables the same. 
The Commission expresses support for the artist's work and robust community outreach plan, 
and notes the importance and challenge of creating a monumental memorial to the abolitionist 
movement in Brooklyn. The project may be resubmitted for review with the understanding that 
the artist will (1) work with the support of the landscape architect to identify additional artwork 
locations, including the site of the fountain, and to ensure the formal expressions of the artwork 
are prominent so the artwork becomes a focal point (or focal points) within the park; (2) 
consider legibility and long-term durability and maintenance of the text installations, in 
particular at locations where people will be sitting or walking; and (3) develop the content with 
input from community members, as proposed. Commissioner Aarons and Commissioner 
Armstead recused themselves. 
 
 
 
 
A true copy of resolution adopted by 
the Design Commission at its meeting 
on January 19, 2021.
