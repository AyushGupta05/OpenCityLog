CERTIFICATE  30316 
 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the reconstruction of 
River Run Playground, Riverside Park, Riverside Drive between West 81st Street and West 
83rd Street, Manhattan, submitted by the Department of Parks & Recreation, represented by 
exhibits 1914-CP, CQ, CR & CS of record in this matter, hereby gives to the same unanimous 
preliminary approval with the understanding that the team will (1) enlarge the individual planting 
bed located north of the 5-to-12-year-old playground and (2) activate the perimeter path with 
elements such as backless benches or painted games, while maintaining the required clearance 
for maintenance vehicles. 
 
Preliminary approval is conditioned upon submission of this project for final review and approval 
before February 23, 2028.
