after-hours. Commissioners also note that any significant changes to the design should be 
submitted for review in advance of a final submission.

before December 16, 2026.

CERTIFICATE  29855 
 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the construction of the 
Soundview Park Recreation Center, Soundview Park, Lafayette Avenue between Morrison 
Avenue and Metcalf Avenue, Bronx, submitted by the Department of Design and Construction 
and the Department of Parks & Recreation, represented by exhibits 2419-HZ, IA, IB, IC & ID of 
record in this matter, hereby gives to the same unanimous preliminary approval with the 
understanding that, in an interim submission before returning for final review, the team will (1) 
clarify the proposed materials for the building, including by (a) studying the reflectivity of the 
metal panels for the upper levels and (b) providing physical material samples; (2) provide a 
planted buffer between the parking lot fencing and the surrounding pedestrian and recreation 
spaces; (3) study further integrating the NYC Parks signage with the architecture; and (4) per 
standard procedure for the Percent for Art component, allow the selected artist further flexibility 
in siting the artwork, noting that the Design Commission discourages design teams from 
prescribing artwork locations and mediums.   
 
Preliminary approval is conditioned upon submission of this project for final review and approval 
before December 16, 2026. 
 
 
 
 
A true copy of resolution adopted by 
the Design Commission at its meeting  
on December 16, 2024.
