CERTIFICATE  27000 
 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered a proposal for the installation of 
the Elizabeth Cady Stanton and Susan B. Anthony Monument by Meredith Bergmann, Literary 
Walk, The Mall, Central Park, Manhattan, submitted by the Department of Parks & Recreation, 
represented by exhibits 7422-A, B & D of record in this matter, hereby gives to the same 
unanimous conceptual approval with the understanding that (1) the artist will (a) work on 
refining the anatomy and proportions of each of the two figures, (b) restudy the gaze of the two 
women, (c) simplify the pedestal design so that the surface is flush, (d) eliminate the 
rectangular base or revise it to an oval to match the pedestal, and (e) integrate the bronze into 
the top of the base and (2) the team will submit revised pedestal text, including the birth and 
death dates for the two women and alternative quotes.  
 
The Commission appreciates that this monument will commemorate two women who were 
pioneering activists of the Woman Suffrage Movement. The Commission also acknowledges 
Elizabeth Cady Stanton and Susan B. Anthony's complicated history, and that the 
contributions and struggles of the many women of color who played a role in this Movement 
remain overlooked. 
 
With this in mind, the Commission gives approval conditioned upon the understanding that, 
separate from the statue of Elizabeth Cady Stanton and Susan B. Anthony, the applicant will 
work to identify meaningful ways to acknowledge and commemorate women of color who 
played an active role in the Woman Suffrage Movement. 
 
Conceptual approval is conditioned upon submission of this project for preliminary review and 
approval before March 18, 2021. 
 
A true copy of resolution adopted by 
the Design Commission at its meeting 
on March 18, 2019. 
 
 
 
 
 
Justin Garrett Moore 
Executive Director
