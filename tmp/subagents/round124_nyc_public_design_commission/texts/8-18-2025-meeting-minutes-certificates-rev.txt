CERTIFICATE  30137 
 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the reconstruction of 
the bandshell and construction of an adjacent shade structure, as part of the reconstruction of 
Damrosch Park, Lincoln Center for the Performing Arts, West 62nd Street between Columbus 
Avenue and Amsterdam Avenue, Manhattan, submitted by the Department of Parks & 
Recreation and the Department of Transportation, represented by exhibits 3470-HF, HG & HH 
of record in this matter, hereby gives to the same unanimous preliminary approval with the 
understanding that in a month, the team shall submit for interim review during the October 2025 
project review cycle, provide responses and drawings that are responsive to the following 
conditions of approval, including to (1) continue to develop a unified language for the 
architectural elements in Damrosch Park, editing the design across the campus to simplify and 
reduce the disparate elements, materials, and geometries; (2) rethink the proportions and 
relationship of the bandshell canopy, the overlook trellis structure, and the steps to create a 
gateway into the plaza, and include (a) transverse and longitudinal sections through both, and 
(b) renderings from a pedestrian perspective to show how both structures are experienced 
simultaneously; (3) develop the design to allow spatial flexibility for equitable daily use, including 
to (a) study how to provide ADA access to the seating area at the southwest stoop to provide an 
equitable, elevated vantage point toward the park and street, (b) include ADA companion 
seating throughout, and (c) reduce any trip hazards and accessibility or circulation challenges at 
the overlook steps; (4) at the overlook trellis structure, (a) remove the tension cables and vines 
from the trellis, (b) redesign the trellis for improved shading for the seating beneath, (c) provide 
detailed drawings showing any control booths, audio-visual, and lighting equipment, and (d) 
provide seating diagrams that clearly illustrate circulation clearances and the relation of the 
seating area to the adjacent grove; (5) refine the West 62nd Street bandshell façade to create a 
more welcoming street wall condition and entrance into Damrosch Park, including to (a) 
investigate opportunities to provide public amenities with streetscape design elements and (b) 
consider allowing the tiered stoop seating to wrap the corner of the building along West 62nd 
Street to provide a more welcoming frontage toward the university campus; and (6) address 
additional site-specific feedback noted by commissioners, including but not limited to concerns 
that (a) the plaza needs to accommodate operational requirements for performances and 
everyday uses at all scales, (b) the deep cavity space under the proscenium stage needs to be 
resolved, and any panels or guardrails must be shown on drawings, (c) the complexity and 
heaviness of the bandshell roof needs to be further studied, lightened, and simplified, (d) any 
rooftop maintenance and safety measures must be considered and included on the drawings,  
and (e) the backstage deck materials and seating types should be simplified and further 
integrated with consideration to the overall campus design. Additionally, in two months, for the 
November 2025 project review cycle, the team shall submit the full site-wide design package, 
including any updates to the bandshell, canopies, terraces, and any other structures or site 
elements, for a holistic preliminary review.
