a portion of John Allen Payne Playground, including the construction of a skate park, Third 
Avenue between 64th Street and 65th Street, Brooklyn, submitted by the Department of Parks & 
Recreation, represented by exhibits 2501-J, K, L & M of record in this matter, hereby gives to 
the same unanimous preliminary approval with the understanding that the team will (1) provide 
drawings showing the relationship between 64th Street, the perimeter chain link fence, and the 
triangular wall hip skate ramp, including section drawings and a perspective drawing that 
depicts the rear of the hip ramp, as viewed from 64th Street; (2) consider adding shock-
absorbent wraps around the column bases for enhanced user safety; (3) consider (a) replacing 
sections of chain link fence with a different type of fence to minimize the confining visual impact, 
while still addressing operational needs, and (b) adding creative details to the 6’H chain link 
fence that separates the skatepark from the park’s central path; (4)(a) clarify what the national 
lighting standard is for skatepark facilities and (b) whether the light intensity and/or illuminance 
levels need to be increased within the skatepark area, especially for use after sunset; and (5) 
consider specifying different tree species that are more resilient, especially with those that can 
tolerate a wide range of soil pH levels.  
 
Preliminary approval is conditioned upon submission of this project for final review and approval 
before April 20, 2028. 
 
A true copy of resolution adopted by 
the Design Commission at its meeting
