RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the installation of 
façade graphics for a stadium, as part of Phase II of Willets Point, 126th Street, 127th Street, 
and Willets Point Boulevard between 38th Avenue and 35th Avenue, Corona, Queens, 
submitted by the Economic Development Corporation, represented by exhibits 7716-BD & BE of 
record in this matter, hereby gives to the same unanimous preliminary approval with the 
understanding that the team will, in an interim submission before returning for final review, 
continue to (1) study the material palette, (a) considering whether broadcasting or aggregate 
additions to the concrete can help increase contrast and (b) providing updated renderings that 
more accurately demonstrate the materials; (2) explore adding iconography related to Queens, 
considering whether the skyline or reference to some other geographic context may be more 
appropriate; (3) study the northwest wall letter forms, considering (a) how the letter forms at this 
wall can be brought into alignment with the typeface used for the monogram and (b) how the 
kerning and spacing can be further refined; (4) consider how the pigeon figure reads at the 
southeast pigeon wall, including (a) whether illustrating the bird as a hero is appropriate and 
contextual and (b) whether the general public will understand the team’s intended message and 
understand the connection between the pigeon and the stadium; and (5) at the northeast flag 
wall, study (a) the recognizability and legibility of the monogram and (b) the removal of the 
second, smaller flag. Commissioner Tisch recused herself.

before February 24, 2029
