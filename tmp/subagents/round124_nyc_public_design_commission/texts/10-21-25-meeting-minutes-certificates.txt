CERTIFICATE  30190 
 
RESOLVED That the Design Commission, having considered designs for the design of a 
master plan for the construction of Metropolitan Park (Queens Future), including the 
construction of a gaming facility, resort, retail, and events complex, parking structures, the 
reconstruction of Shea Road, and adjacent site work, Roosevelt, submitted by the Economic 
Development Corporation and the Department of Parks & Recreation, represented by exhibits 
7786-E, F, G, H & I of record in this matter, hereby gives to the same unanimous preliminary 
approval, subject to the conditions listed below. Commissioner Castilla recused herself. 
Commissioner Armstead voted to abstain, citing too many unknowns.  
 
This approval is conditioned upon the following requests for design development and 
clarifications, which must be included in amended preliminary submissions prior to proceeding 
to final review: 
 
(1) For the Integrated Resort: (a) mitigate the public space impact from the approximately 50 
relocated parking spots on Roosevelt Avenue, potentially redistributing the spaces away from 
the pedestrian frontage; (b) continue to design the daycare center, including the façade 
treatment and the access and design details of the outdoor playground, including how this 
space interfaces with the adjacent public realm; (c) provide information on the proposed 
smoking terrace and cigar bar, including how their impacts can be mitigated at major public 
access points and gathering spaces; (d) continue the design development of the mezzanine-
level promenade at the east colonnade, particularly as it relates to accessible circulation; and 
the (e) continue to study the design of the northern surface lot, including to reduce the quantity 
of garage entrances, to rationalize vehicle circulation, and to mitigate the impacts from the one-
way bus and loading route exit on Metropolitan Park. 
 
(2 & 3) For the Northfield Garage and Southfield Garage: (a) refine the ground plane design, 
including to provide additional activated façades and fencing details, especially at the interface 
with the Willets Point neighborhood; and (b) at both the Metropolitan Park and Seaver Way 
locations, continue to refine the circulation lobbies and elevated connection spaces, considering 
the public interface and anti-climb features.

(4) For the Taste of Queens building: (a) refine the architectural transition between the market 
and garage massing to avoid an abrupt condition; (b) for the market space, continue to develop 
the architecture more specifically in relation to the food hall and community space programs; (c) 
continue to design how the lobby interfaces with Roosevelt Avenue in a publicly welcoming 
manner; (d) clarify the service access for loading and deliveries to the market; (e) for the back-
of-house support space for the New York Mets, refine the public realm interface and create 
active public-facing storefronts; and (f) clarify how the space will be accessed by services 
vehicles through the garage without necessitating additional driveways or vehicular entrances. 
 
(5) For the public realm framework: (a) continue to design Southfield Park and add a public 
restroom facility south of Roosevelt Avenue in coordination with NYC Parks; (b) continue to 
develop the design of the Northern & Southern Gateway bridges and the MTA Station 
improvements in coordination with the MTA, NYC DOT, and other agencies holding jurisdiction; 
and (c) continue to refine the design of the restroom facilities, security booths, and any other 
architectural structures and site furnishings throughout the park.  
 
In addition, any artwork proposed at the site should be coordinated with the Department of 
Cultural Affairs’ Percent for Art team before a process for the artist or artwork selection is 
determined.  
 
Preliminary approval is conditioned upon submission of this project for final review and approval 
before October 21, 2027.
