# Round 126 London Planning Appeals / Called-In More

Scratch-only output for additional London architecture-related administrative milestones from official GOV.UK, MHCLG, Planning Casework Unit, HS2/GOV.UK, and Planning Inspectorate sources.

## Method

- Read existing corpus/source registry references for `mhclg-called-in-decisions` and screened known duplicate source URLs/case refs.
- Used GOV.UK collection lines and publication pages for London called-in and recovered-appeal records.
- Added a small number of official Planning Inspectorate appeal PDFs to reach 30 candidates where GOV.UK collection records not already in the corpus were limited.
- Kept all events as administrative milestones, with explicit limitations that they are not construction, opening, completion, occupation, or outcome evidence.

## Duplicate Handling

Excluded clear existing corpus items such as Poplar Business Park, London City Airport 2016/2024, former Hackney Police Station, 843 London Road, William Sutton Estate, 1 Cambridge Heath Road, Chiswick Roundabout, 214 Tunnel Avenue, Harrow School, Westferry Printworks, North London Business Park, 10 King Henry's Road, Burgess Business Park, Notting Hill Gate, Rosehill, 40/40A High Street Brentford, Whitechapel Bell Foundry, 8 Albert Embankment, Victoria Tower Gardens, 20 Bury Street, Edith Summerskill House, Windsor Grove, 103-111a High Street Croydon, New City Court, Broadway Retail Park, Homebase/Tesco Osterley, London Television Centre, 225 Marsh Wall, 456-472 Oxford Street 2024, and Royal Mint Court.

## Caveats

- Several 2014 GOV.UK collection-log entries are useful discovery candidates but should not be promoted without archived/FOI decision letters or PDFs.
- Coordinates are approximate address or area points for atlas discovery, not surveyed geometries or site boundaries.
- Planning Inspectorate ordinary appeal examples are lower signal than Secretary of State/called-in decisions; they are included only as official observed administrative milestones.
