# Product Principles

Bims-5 should become a public-interest city change atlas: a transparent, citable record of urban change.

## Mission

Show what changed in a city, when it changed, where it changed, and what evidence supports it.

## Non-Goals

- Do not build a fake-heavy planning simulator.
- Do not imply 10-year predictions.
- Do not launder correlation into causation.
- Do not present OSM edit history as direct evidence of real-world construction dates.
- Do not use generic AI impact scores as planner evidence.
- Do not ship flashy demos that make evidence harder to inspect.

## User Value

The project should help users:

- Explore observed urban change by area, date, category, and source.
- Understand confidence and limitations.
- Export evidence briefs for discussion or review.
- Report corrections and add better sources.
- Compare proposed changes to historical analogues without pretending to predict outcomes.

## Evidence Standard

Every claim should answer:

- What is the source?
- Who published it?
- What license governs it?
- When was it accessed?
- What date does it describe?
- What geography does it cover?
- What transformation produced the displayed artifact?
- What uncertainty or gap remains?

## Language Standard

Prefer:

- "Observed change."
- "Historical analogue."
- "Confidence and limitations."
- "Descriptive, not predictive."
- "Evidence-backed event."

Avoid:

- "Forecast."
- "Predict."
- "Will cause."
- "10-year simulation."
- "AI-powered impact."
- "Proof" unless there is external audit evidence.
